Our paper "Low-Frequency First: Eliminating Floating Artifacts in 3D Gaussian Splatting" has received **CW2025 Best Paper Honorable Mention**!

From October 14 to 16, 2025, I attended the 24th International Conference on Cyberworlds in Jinzhong. I presented the paper at the conference and our paper was awarded the **Best Paper Honorable Mention**.
