Projects

  • Error propagation in bundle adjustment (EPEA-BA)
    Developed a GPU solver for approximate bundle adjustment at Westlake University.
    • Traced how elimination order and viewing geometry govern error transfer and quantified local recovery on a fixed linearization.
    • Fit cameras independently and used residuals from all observing views and curvature-corrected Adam to update each 3D point.
    • Met the reprojection target on 16 of 20 scenes under matched budgets. On the largest BAL scene, time to target was 4.92 seconds.
  • 2026.05 - 2026.10
    Video Virtual Try-On
    Designed a two-stage video try-on pipeline at Westlake University and built the human animation prototype.
    • Reproduced OmniTryOn and ViViD and found cases where the original hemline or collar survived the edit. I designed the pipeline around a single dressed reference image so I could check the garment before animating the wearer.
    • Curated 3.2 hours of real footage from four fashion datasets and rendered about 10 hours of synchronized Mixamo pairs in Blender, with the same action performed by characters of different proportions.
    • Implemented separate appearance and motion inputs on Wan2.2. Following IM-Animation, I trained the motion retargeter for 40,000 steps on two GPUs, then fine-tuned the video generator for 20,000 steps on one GPU.
